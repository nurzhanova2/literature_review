~~~text
borrower data → risk model → explanation → early warning → expert review
~~~

## Research Question

> **How effectively can explainable machine-learning models identify latent deterioration in borrower quality before formal default under an out-of-time validation design, when assessed jointly for discrimination, calibration, warning lead time, false-alert burden, and the temporal stability of explanations?**

## Scope

- Borrower-level credit-risk monitoring;
- latent deterioration defined independently of the candidate model;
- comparison of interpretable baselines and flexible ML models;
- evaluation of discrimination, calibration, lead time, false-alert burden, and explanation stability.
