# Detailed Comparative Table

## Framework for Comparison with the Proposed Research Design

In the final column, the term **proposed design** does not refer to an already proven, completed model. It refers to the intended dissertation design: prediction at the **individual-borrower** level; early detection of **latent deterioration before formal default**; a dynamic or survival formulation where appropriate; calibrated PD; measurement of lead time and false alarms; out-of-time testing without leakage; local explanations and assessment of their stability over time; and decision logging and governance.
